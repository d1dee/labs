The problem

Your computer lab is unmanaged

Most school computer labs in Kenya operate without any central control. The teacher walks around the room to check what students are doing, manually shuts down machines one by one, and has no way to block access to distracting sites during lessons. During KNEC preparation, there is no inexpensive way to format computers to KNEC compliant standards.

No visibility
You cannot see what each student is doing without physically walking to every desk.
Uncontrolled internet
Students browse YouTube, social media, and gaming sites during computer lessons.
No content filtering
No firewall or domain blocking. Any site is accessible, including inappropriate content.
Expensive Exam Preparation
Every exam cycle, schools pay contractors to manually prepare and format machines for KNEC exams one by one.
The solution

Three pillars of lab control

Everything a school needs to manage, protect, and lock down a computer lab — from a single browser dashboard. No software to install on student machines. Works with your existing network.

Lab Management
One click KNEC exam formatting, remote shutdown, bulk file copying, and bulk actions across all machines.
Internet Control
Toggle internet access for the whole lab or individual machines with one click.
Firewall & Blocking
Block specific domains, wildcard patterns, or entire categories of content.

Context

Why this matters now

The CBC curriculum mandates Digital Literacy from Grade 1 onwards. Every school is expected to have a functional computer lab with supervised, structured access. The government is auditing ICT infrastructure. Schools without proper lab management will not meet compliance requirements — and learners are not getting the intended value from computer sessions.

Labs FCD was built to solve this specific gap. It does not require expensive hardware, dedicated IT staff, or enterprise-grade networking equipment. It works with the computers and network you already have.
